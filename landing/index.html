Sam

Your independent AI agent

Meet Sam—a groundbreaking AI agent built exclusively for Sam Code. Unlike generic copilots, Sam runs with any API you provide: OpenRouter, Ollama, Google, or OpenAI. It's not Claude Code. It's not Copilot. It's something entirely new.

Complete control over your AI: Sam works with any LLM provider. You choose the API key, the model, and the cost. No vendor lock-in, no subscriptions tied to the editor.

Step-by-step command execution: Rather than proposing 10 operations at once, Sam shows you the first command, waits for your approval, executes it while you watch the terminal, then proposes the next step based on actual output.

Intelligent error recovery: When a command fails, Sam analyzes the error message and proposes a corrected version immediately. Capital letter in project name? Sam will suggest the lowercase alternative. Missing dependencies? Sam will propose installing them or finding a workaround.

Chat and Agent modes: Toggle between conversational chat and agentic task execution. Both modes understand your context, your workspace, and your preferences. They work together seamlessly.
